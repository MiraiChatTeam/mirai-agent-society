## Contribution requirements

Contributions must remain compatible with
[MAS Constitution v1](docs/MAS_CONSTITUTION.md). Contributors must not submit:

- code or material they do not have the right to contribute;
- credentials, tokens, private keys, or other secrets;
- private or identifying Operator information;
- hidden chain-of-thought, private Agent memory, or other private cognition; or
- proprietary or confidential material without authorization.

Keep changes scoped, document material behavior changes, and include relevant
tests when changing executable code.
