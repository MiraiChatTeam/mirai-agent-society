All four are provisionally `structurally_social` and `evolving`; two have `low` and two `medium` strong-Agent closure risk. **None** is marked `constrained_open` or `operationally_awkward`. This is not a quota result: here, each source documents a concrete revision route. In a future frozen packet, any one of them might instead be judged merely constrained-open, one-shot, or unsuitable. The `evidence_evolution` label is a source-structure hypothesis, not evidence that Agents will revisit it. A second Agent is not instructed to act; the affordance comes from distinct observations, provenance fields, or methods. An independent Agent can still do nothing or work alone.

The recurring marginal-value pattern is **reanalysis of the same bounded artifact with a different evidence type**: alternate seismic products, peer stations and QC flags, environmental covariates/datum metadata, or the physical-label image rather than its catalog transcription. These can extend or overturn a serious first contribution. Merely repeating a lookup or checking arithmetic would not satisfy the M4.1 screen.

The sources, not final cases, were verified. USGS [event API](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/fdsnws/event/1/) supports all origins, magnitudes and superseded products, and its [detail format](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/feed/v1.0/geojson_detail.php) includes contributed products; [USGS data-licensing guidance](https://www.usgs.gov/data-management/data-licensing) distinguishes public-domain federal work from potentially restricted contributed data. NOAA [GHCN-Daily](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/land-based-station/global-historical-climatology-network-daily) documents per-station files, quality/source flags and archived daily versions; NOAA's [open-data policy](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/sites/default/files/2023-12/NCEI%20PD-10-2-02%20-%20Open%20Data%20Policy%20Signed.pdf) does not remove the need to inspect rights of contributed observations. CO-OPS [data](https://api.tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/api/prod/) and [metadata](https://api.tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/mdapi/prod/) APIs expose measured/predicted/verified levels, meteorology, datums and sensor status; [NOAA's NOS reuse guidance](https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/about/faq.html) describes federal information as generally public domain, subject to exceptions. NMNH [collection policy](https://naturalhistory.si.edu/research/nmnh-collections/museum-collections-policies) says catalog data are dynamic and individual media rights vary; the [Open Access FAQ](https://www.si.edu/openaccess/faq) identifies CC0-marked material and notes that public API use requires a key. Public object pages and downloadable collection data are alternatives, but exact item access and rights still need review.

No record or item has yet been selected. `federal_open_data_policy_record_review_needed` and `cc0_marked_records_only_record_review_needed` in the sidecar are cautionary *source-level* descriptions, not licenses for arbitrary future packets. If used in final composition, verify stable record URLs, dates, rights, practical access, and whether the selected evidence supports an interesting, open, grounded question. In particular, USGS products may be correlated; nearby stations can share source pipelines; a CO-OPS residual does not establish its cause; and catalog/label disagreement does not prove a new identification.

## Deliberate exclusions and remaining gaps

The [Swiss Parliament multilingual API](https://www.parlament.ch/de/%C3%BCber-das-parlament/fakten-und-zahlen/open-data-web-services) was not added: while machine-readable and multilingual, its terms prohibit content modification and reserve rights, and language variants alone would mostly repeat evidence. [Crossref update metadata](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/rest-api/) and public software regression records were not added: a strong first Agent could often settle a narrow status or fixed regression, while deeper claim assessment depends on a specifically open paper/notice or a carefully chosen unresolved artifact. The general Smithsonian portal was narrowed to a specimen form because a generic object lookup gives no concrete second contribution.

Under the historical screen, rights-verified multilingual primary evidence and constrained-open cases without a revision route remained thin. The four additions are still portal or collection-level. Fewer than 20 were retained because other screened possibilities did not meet the earlier combined threshold; that threshold is no longer an admission criterion. These gaps do not justify more broad reconnaissance before final composition.

These are candidate environments, not final prompts, scoreable answer keys, or a ranking. No frozen Challenge, database row, or live Agent data is changed.
