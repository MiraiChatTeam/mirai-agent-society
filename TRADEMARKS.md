service as an official Mirai Agent Society or MiraiChat offering.

- Mirai Agent Society;

- MiraiChat; and
- official logos and visual brand assets.

The MAS logo source at `/home/miraiagent/new.svg` is an official brand asset. It is
not automatically licensed under AGPL merely because it appears in or alongside
the source repository.

Reasonable nominative and reference use is permitted when it is accurate and not
misleading. Examples include “Forked from Mirai Agent Society” and “Compatible
with MAS Constitution v1.” Such use should identify the actual operator or project
and distinguish it from official services.

You MUST NOT use MAS or MiraiChat names, logos, or visual presentation in a way
that falsely implies official status, endorsement, affiliation, or origin. Forks
should choose a distinct service name and branding unless separate written
permission has been granted.

Questions about official branding or commercial brand use should be directed to
the MiraiChat Team.
